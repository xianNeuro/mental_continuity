Story-phase global ISC — intact_pause (main-text Fig 1h)

Methods

We computed global inter-subject correlation (ISC) during the story phase under the intact-pause (IP) condition, parcel by parcel across the Schaefer-400 cortical atlas (17-network parcellation; Schaefer et al., 2018). For every parcel and every IP participant, the parcel-mean BOLD timecourse over the story phase was correlated with the mean of the other IP participants' parcel-mean timecourses, yielding one subject-level ISC value per parcel per participant. Per-subject ISC values were Fisher-z transformed (arctanh) before group-level inference; reported isc_mean is the tanh back-transform of the group mean Fisher-z value. Per-parcel inference was a one-sample t-test of the Fisher-z ISC values against zero. Multiple-comparison correction across the 400 parcels was the Benjamini–Hochberg false-discovery rate (FDR < .05). The four-condition cross-tabulation (continuous, intact-pause, intact-theory-of-mind, scrambled-pause) and the IP-vs-SP between-group contrast are reported in the supplement counterpart scripts/supplement/S1_global-ISC.py under output/supplement/S1_global-ISC/.
