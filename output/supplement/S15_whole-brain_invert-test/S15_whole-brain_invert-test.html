Supplementary Section S15 (whole-brain): story-to-interruption inversion and invert-selectivity maps

Methods

The story-to-interruption inversion test of Result 3.1 and the invert-selectivity test of Result 3.2 were repeated at every Schaefer-400 parcel for four inter-subject schemes: three within-condition (IP-IP, SP-SP, IT-IT) and one across-condition (IP-IT, each intact-pause participant compared with the intact-theory-of-mind group). For every parcel and every interruption epoch we built two 10-TR template multivoxel patterns: a story window (the ten TRs ending one TR before interruption onset) and an interruption window (the ten TRs starting five TRs after onset). The story-interruption ISPC at every ordered pair of epochs (i, j) was a single Pearson correlation between one participant's story-window template at epoch i and the comparison group's averaged interruption-window template at epoch j (leave-one-out within-condition; full-cohort mean for IP-IT). The inversion statistic for each parcel and scheme is the group t against zero of the matching diagonal of this ISPC, in the family's expected NEGATIVE direction (pattern reversal across the interruption). The invert-selectivity statistic is the paired t of subject (mean matching − mean mismatching) values against zero, in the same NEGATIVE direction (the inversion is epoch-specific when matching is more negative than mismatching). Per-parcel statistics are listed in data/parcel_results.csv.

Two brain-map rows are shown, with the posterior medial cortex (PMC) region of interest outlined in black on every map so the whole-brain statistics can be read against the PMC location. Row 1 shows the inversion t-statistic at every parcel that passes own-condition reliability (Result 2.1 jackknife sign-flip p < 0.05) AND the inversion jackknife sign-flip permutation p < 0.05 in the predicted negative direction; reliability values come from S6_whole-brain-analysis/data/parcel_results.csv. Row 2 shows the invert-selectivity t-statistic at every parcel whose matching-vs-mismatching permutation reaches an uncorrected p < 0.10 in the predicted negative direction (matching more inverted than mismatching).

Both rows use the project’s high-resolution four-view cortical surface helper on the inflated fsaverage mesh (lateral and medial views of each hemisphere), with the project’s native red−blue diverging palette centered at 0 and the PMC region drawn as a black outline.
