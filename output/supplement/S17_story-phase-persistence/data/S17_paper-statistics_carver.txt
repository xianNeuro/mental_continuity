Section S17 — Story-phase PMC pattern persistence predicting neural
resumption (DMN realignment, excl. PMC) and later narrative recall.

Pooled IP+IT+SP OLS; condition fixed effect; per-participant observations.
Story-phase persistence:        the ten TRs ending one TR before interruption onset.
Interruption-phase persistence: the ten TRs beginning five TRs after onset (Result 4).
Across participants, story-phase and interruption-phase persistence correlate at r = 0.711 (n = 57).
