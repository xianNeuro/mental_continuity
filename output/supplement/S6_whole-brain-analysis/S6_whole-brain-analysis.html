Supplementary Section S6 (whole-brain): selectivity and evolve maps

Methods

The pattern-similarity reliability, selectivity, and evolve analyses reported for posterior medial cortex (PMC) in the main text (Result 2.1, Result 2.2 and Result 2.3) were repeated at every Schaefer-400 parcel. For every parcel we computed the same per-participant inter-subject pattern correlation (15-second analysis window beginning 7.5 s after interruption onset, the first five TRs from onset discarded to avoid hemodynamic carry-over). Reliability inference follows Result 2.1: a one-sided sign-flip permutation test on subject pseudo-values from a delete-one-subject jackknife on the Fisher-z group mean ISPC (a parcel passes when pperm < 0.05 in the expected positive direction). Selectivity is the participant-level mean matching minus mean mismatching ISPC; evolve is the per-participant OLS slope of mean ISPC against forward epoch distance d = 1..8. Per-parcel inferential statistics for all three tests — reliability θ̂z, SE, bootstrap CI, sign-flip permutation p; selectivity mean diff, t, within-subject label-shuffle permutation p, bootstrap CI; evolve slope, within-subject temporal-label permutation p — are listed in data/parcel_results.csv.

Two brain-map rows are shown, with the posterior medial cortex (PMC) region of interest outlined in black on every map so the whole-brain statistics can be read against the PMC location. Row 1 shows the t-statistic at every parcel that passes own-condition reliability (pperm < 0.05) for five (condition × measure) cells: IP-IP selectivity, IT-IP selectivity, IP-IP evolve, IT-IP evolve, and SP-SP evolve. Row 2 shows the same t-statistic at every parcel whose own permutation test reaches an uncorrected p < 0.10 in the hypothesised direction: the selectivity panels retain parcels with positive selectivity (matching > mismatching), and the evolve panels (including the SP-SP evolve control) retain parcels with a negative slope (pattern similarity declining with epoch distance). Rows 1 and 2 share column layout exactly so each column compares the reliability set against the uncorrected-significant set within the same (condition × measure) cell.

Both rows use the project’s high-resolution four-view cortical surface helper on the inflated fsaverage mesh (lateral and medial views of each hemisphere), with the project’s native red−blue diverging palette centered at 0 and the PMC region drawn as a black outline.
